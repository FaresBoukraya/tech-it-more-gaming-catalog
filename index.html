Questions, answered

Do you sell these products?

No. Tech It More is an independent research tool. Each product links to the retailers' own pages, and you buy from them directly.

Why don't you show prices?

Prices and stock change constantly, and the site has no approved retailer feed. Instead of showing numbers that may be out of date, it sends you to the store to see the real price, stock and sales.

Do store links confirm that a product is in stock?

No. Each link opens a search for the product's brand and model. Only the store's own page can confirm availability.

Where is my watchlist stored?

Only in this browser. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded, so clearing your browser data also clears the watchlist. Use Export to keep a copy.

Are you affiliated with the brands or stores shown?

No. Brand and store names and product photos belong to their owners and appear only to help you find products.
