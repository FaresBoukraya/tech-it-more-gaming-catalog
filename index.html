Questions, answered

What is Tech It More, and how does it make money?

It is an independent catalog. It sells nothing, runs no ads, and has no affiliate tags. If that changes, affiliate links will be labelled clearly. We don't quote prices ourselves: store links open each US retailer's own search page, which is the only place that can confirm price and stock.

Wireless or wired for competitive play?

A 2.4GHz dongle is generally as responsive as a cable for most players, and usually lower latency than Bluetooth. Pick wired if you never want to think about charging.

What does "typical price tier" mean?

Budget, mid-range or premium is a rough guide to where a product sits among similar gear. It is not a price. Check the store for what it costs today.

What leaves my browser?

Product photos load from manufacturers' servers. Opening a product may fetch extra photos from Wikimedia Commons. There are no accounts, trackers or ads. Your saved list stays in this browser only.

Where is my saved list stored?

Only in this browser, so clearing browser data clears it. Use "Copy link to saved list" to share it, or Export my data to keep a copy.
