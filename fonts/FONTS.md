# Fonts

Both are self-hosted (no Google Fonts request) and under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: https://scripts.sil.org/OFL

- **Carlito** (body text), regular and bold, Latin subset. Copyright 2013 The Carlito Project Authors (https://github.com/googlefonts/carlito)
- **Lora** (headings and logo), Latin subset.

The Carlito files were subset from the TrueType originals with fontTools (`pyftsubset`), keeping Latin, punctuation, arrows and the inch mark.
