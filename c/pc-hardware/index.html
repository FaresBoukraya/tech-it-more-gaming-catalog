PCs, monitors and components

Prebuilt PCs, gaming monitors and parts such as graphics cards, for desktops and desk setups.

What to check before you buy

  • For a monitor, match resolution to your graphics card first, then pick the refresh rate your games can actually reach.
  • Check a graphics card against your case length and power supply before buying; models differ in size and power connector.
  • A prebuilt saves time but usually costs more than the same parts bought separately.
  • Panel type changes the look: OLED has the deepest blacks, IPS is brighter and avoids the burn-in concern.
