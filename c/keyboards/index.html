Gaming keyboards

Mechanical, optical and analog gaming keyboards in full-size, tenkeyless and compact layouts.

What to check before you buy

  • Pick the switch type you like: linear for smooth presses, tactile for a bump, clicky for audible feedback.
  • Choose a size that fits your desk and mouse space. Tenkeyless and 75% boards leave more room for mouse movement.
  • For wireless boards, a 2.4GHz connection is usually lower latency than Bluetooth.
  • Analog or magnetic switches with adjustable actuation and rapid trigger are mainly useful in fast competitive shooters.
