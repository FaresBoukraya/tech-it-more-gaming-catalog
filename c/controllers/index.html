Gaming controllers

Gamepads and controllers for consoles and PC.

What to check before you buy

  • Make sure the controller supports your platform: Xbox, PlayStation, Switch and PC are not all interchangeable.
  • Wireless is convenient, but check the battery life and whether it connects by Bluetooth or a dedicated dongle.
  • Stick drift is a common failure on analog sticks. Models with Hall effect sticks avoid the part that usually wears out.
  • Back paddles and button remapping are worth having if you play competitively.
