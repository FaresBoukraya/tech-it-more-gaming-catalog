Gaming consoles, handhelds and VR headsets

Consoles, handheld gaming PCs and VR headsets. Prices and bundles change often, so use the store links to compare the current offers.

What to check before you buy

  • Check which game library you are buying into. Console games, PC storefronts and VR stores are largely separate.
  • Confirm the exact model and edition. Many consoles come in several storage sizes and bundles.
  • Modern games are large, so storage size and expandability matter more than they used to.
  • For handhelds, look at battery life and screen type as well as raw performance.
