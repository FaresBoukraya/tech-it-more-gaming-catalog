Streaming and audio gear

Capture cards, webcams, microphones and speakers for streaming and desk setups.

What to check before you buy

  • Check the connection: capture cards and webcams need a fast USB port, and some will not work on a hub.
  • A USB microphone is simpler than an XLR setup, but it picks up keyboard noise; position it close and use a pop filter.
  • For a capture card, compare the pass-through resolution with what it can record. They are often different.
  • For webcams, sensor size and lighting matter more than the headline resolution.
