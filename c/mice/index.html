Gaming mice

Wired and wireless gaming mice, from ultralight esports shapes to ergonomic designs.

What to check before you buy

  • Shape and size matter more than the sensor spec. Match the mouse to your hand size and grip style.
  • Lighter mice are easier to flick, but some people prefer a heavier, more stable feel.
  • Sensors on current gaming mice are all accurate enough for most players, so a bigger DPI number is not a reason to pay more.
  • For wireless mice, check battery life and whether the receiver supports a high polling rate.
