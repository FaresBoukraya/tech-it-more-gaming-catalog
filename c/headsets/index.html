Gaming headsets

Wired and wireless gaming headsets with built-in or detachable microphones.

What to check before you buy

  • Check platform support. A wireless dongle that works on PC may not work on every console.
  • Comfort over long sessions depends on weight, clamping force and ear cup size.
  • If voice chat matters, look for a detachable or boom microphone and read reviews of the mic specifically.
  • For wireless models, compare battery life and whether the headset can be used while charging.
