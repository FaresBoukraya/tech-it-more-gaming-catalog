How we choose

Tech It More is a catalog of gaming gear with a short, plain verdict on each product. These are buying notes, not tested reviews. Here is what goes into one, and what does not.

What each entry contains

  • Specs taken from the manufacturer's own product page.
  • A photo from the manufacturer's site, with a link back to the source.
  • Best for: the kind of player or setup the product suits.
  • The catch: the main trade-off to know before you pay.
  • Skip it if: the situation where another product is the better buy.
  • Up to three alternatives, each with the reason it is on the list.

What the price tiers mean

Budget, Mid-range and Premium are relative to other gear in the same category. A Premium mouse and a Premium console are very different amounts of money. The tier is a rough guide, not a quote; the retailer shows the real price.

What we do not do

  • We do not test products, score them or rank them.
  • We do not quote prices or confirm stock ourselves. Each store link is a plain search on that retailer's own site.
  • We do not claim a verdict is final. Products get replaced and prices move.

Last checked

When a product shows a "Last checked" date, its specs and claims were compared with the manufacturer's page on that date. A product marked "Not yet re-checked" has not had that pass yet, so confirm key specs with the store or the maker.

What is still missing

The catalog is small, and some brands are better covered than others. Budget picks are thin in several categories. We would rather say so than hide it, and we are adding products category by category.

Spotted a mistake?

Email us a correction with the product name and what is wrong, and we will fix it.
